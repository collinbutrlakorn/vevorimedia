Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Vevori Media (“we”, “us”) collects through vevorimedia.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you use our contact form, we collect the information you type into it: your name, email address, project type, and message. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies on this site, and we do not sell your information.

How we use it

We use what you send us only to reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote, and, if you become a client, run your project. We keep client records for as long as we reasonably need them for business, tax, and legal purposes.

Who handles it

Form submissions pass through Formspree, a form service that delivers them to our inbox. Our email is hosted by Google. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log visitors’ IP addresses and technical details. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when the page loads. These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information you have sent us. Email vevorimedia@gmail.com and we will respond.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.
