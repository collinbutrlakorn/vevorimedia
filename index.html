Common questions

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted individually, based on what we're making and how much shooting and editing it takes. Tell us about yours through the form and we'll get back to you with a quote.

How do monthly retainers work?

You pick a set amount of video each month, and we plan, shoot and edit it on a regular schedule. It's one simple monthly arrangement instead of a new project every time. Tell us what you need and we'll put together a plan and a quote.

How long does it take?

Roughly a few days, depending on the size of the project. We'll agree on a timeline with you before we start.

Do you travel?

Yes. We're based in Oregon and prefer to work within the state, but we're open to traveling farther depending on the scale of the project.

Do we get the raw footage?

No. Like most professional production companies, we deliver the finished edit rather than raw footage. Vevori Media retains ownership of all footage and finished work, and clients are licensed to use the finished video for the purpose we agree on in your quote. If you need something different, ask us before we start.

What equipment do you use?

Professional-grade equipment: camera, lenses, lighting, and microphones. If your project calls for something more, such as aerial footage or a larger crew, tell us up front and we'll let you know honestly whether we can do it.

Do I need a deposit?

Yes. A deposit is required to get a project started, and we'll go over the details when we send your quote.
