Fifteen minutes on what you need, where it's going, and what it costs. You get a written quote and a shoot date the same week.

02

Usually one day. Gear, crew, and permits handled; we work around your schedule and stay out of the way of whatever you're actually running.

Who you're hiring

Vevori Media is Collin Butrlakorn. I grew up in Alaska, outside and playing ball, and the basketball mixtapes I couldn't stop watching are most of why I picked up a camera in the first place. Photography came first, video took over, and the commercial work is now the part I build everything else around.

Right now the company is one person, which is the point: you talk to whoever is holding the camera, from the first email through the final file. When a job needs a second shooter or an audio op, I bring people I've worked with rather than a rotating crew.

Based in Oregon, back in Alaska often enough that a shoot up there isn't a logistics problem. My personal and creative work is at collinmotion.com.
